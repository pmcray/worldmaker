## 3. Five-Epoch Deep Macro-History (-300,000 to 1107)
### Epoch I: The Ancients & Deep Time (`-300,000`)
Grandfather's children occupy Foreven, transplanting the halfling-like Issugur (Homo sapiens parvulus) from Pleistocene Terra to Parthinia (3018) and later The Shire (2835), scattering Droyne (0935, 1216) and Chirpers (0104, 1303), and leaving four devastated Ancient Ruins worlds (1726, 2615, 2740, 3006). Meanwhile, eight native sophont species evolve independently, including the two-headed octopod Tlinzha of Tlesho (0720) and the dormant seven-fold radial Heptamorphoid Hivemind of Septima Prime (1628).

### Epoch II: Zhodani Coreward Wave, Rule of Man & Darrian Contact (`-5000 to -955`)
Zhodani scouts from coreward Ziafrplians explore Iakr (-5000) and settle Subsectors A, B, and C (-4000 to -1000), establishing the provincial capital at Zdovesil (1212) and contacting the Tlinzha (-4880). During the Rule of Man (-1856 to -1850), the Solomani Tvastar-class manufactory starship 'Avalar' settles Avalar (1636) while its companion seedship 'Caelum Felis' lands genetically uplifted Terran cats (Felis catus sapiens) at Bastet (0131). Zhodani explorers contact Avalar in -1635 and introduce psionics (-1000), which the Avalar Church canonizes at the Council of Revelation (-588). To trailing, the pre-Maghiz Darrian Empire contacts native worlds (1927, 2328) before collapsing in the Maghiz solar flare of -955.

### Epoch III: Pre-War Imperial Thin Wave & Spinward Main Extension (`-21 to 589`)
Avalar achieves Jump-1 (-21) and settles Tranquilidad (1637) in Year 18. Imperial scouts from the Spinward Marches reach Avalar in 206. Prior to 600, an Imperial thin settlement wave crosses from Vilis, Darrian, and Five Sisters into the four trailing subsectors—Massina (D), Fessor (H), Reidain (L), and Urnian (P)—extending the Spinward Main through Raschev (3230) and Alenzar (3229) to the Naval/Scout stronghold at Hollis (2523).

### Epoch IV: Frontier Wars I–II & Geopolitical Partition (`589 to 750`)
In the First Frontier War (589–604), Zhodani forces conquer Massina (D), driving out Imperial forces and leaving unabsorbed human colonies under Consular oversight. Following the Second Frontier War (615–620), the Zhodani push a defensive thin settlement wave into Subsectors E, F, and G (600–700) and sign the Treaty of 628 formally establishing the 27-system Avalar Consulate in Subsectors N and O as a psionic client buffer blocking Imperial expansion to spinward.

### Epoch V: Psionic Suppressions, The Septiman War & Eve of the Fifth Frontier War (`800 to 1107`)
The Imperial Psionic Suppressions (800–826) drive a major wave of psionic refugees into the Avalar Consulate. During the Third (979–986) and Fourth (1082–1084) Frontier Wars, Avalar and Imperial forces in Urnian trade bloody raids (devastating Tranquilidad in 985), and in 1045 Avalar occupies the Vargr corsair world of Zuekgoz (1938). Meanwhile, in Subsector J, the seven-fold radial Heptamorphoid Hivemind of Septima Prime (1628)—having stripped the 'Bermuda Triangle' worlds of northern Subsector J (1124–1622) barren to gestate living spore-dreadnoughts—erupts southward in 1104. Now in 1107, as the Fifth Frontier War ignites in the Spinward Marches, Defence Minister Admiral Jorj Valenz's Avalar Consular Armada is locked in desperate combat against the Heptarchy of Septima along the Lun Escarpa (1630) – Rocaroja (1432) – Pres Tinto (1734) fortress line.

## 5. Major & Indigenous Sophont Species
### Uplifted Terran Cats (Felis catus sapiens) (Homeworld Hex `0131`)
* **Morphology**: Bilateral Digitigrade Felid (5–8 kg agile quadrupedal/bipedal form) (4 limbs (forepaws with engineered opposable thumbs, hind leaping legs, balance tail))
* **Senses & Diet**: Tapetum Lucidum Low-Light Vision, Ultrasonic Pinnae Hearing, Vibrissae Micro-Airflow Tactile Array | Obligate Carnivore (cultured gourmet protein & live-hunt preserves)
* **Psychology**: Fiercely independent, intensely curious, nocturnal-crepuscular, high-reflex astrogators
* **Historical Summary**: Arrived aboard the Rule of Man seedship Caelum Felis in -1851; established the high-tech Chatelaine of Bastet (0131, 0232, 0332, 0431, 0825) in spinward-rimward Foreven.

### Tlinzha (Homeworld Hex `0720`)
* **Morphology**: Bilateral Two-Headed Octopod (U-shaped 2m scaled body) (8 limbs (4 central walking legs, 2 arms at Red-Sun Head, 2 arms at White-Sun Head))
* **Senses & Diet**: Red-Head Armored Infrared Eyes, White-Head Retractable Stalked UV-Visual Eyes, Synaesthetic Olfactory-Auditory Pores | Pack Scavenger (governed by the sacred Protocols of the Consumption of Meats)
* **Psychology**: Territorial, ritualistic, dual-hemisphere vigilance; suspicious of offworlders
* **Historical Summary**: Contacted by Zhodani in -4880; reverse-engineered Jump-1 drive from a wrecked Imperial trader by 300; rule the xenophobic 4-system Tlesho Union (0720, 0819, 0820, 0919).

### Zhodani (Humaniti) (Homeworld Hex `1212`)
* **Morphology**: Bilateral (Tall Humaniti) (4 limbs (2 arms, 2 legs))
* **Senses & Diet**: Standard Human Senses, Trained Psionic Talents | Omnivore
* **Psychology**: Harmonious, three-caste psionic society (Nobles, Intendants, Proles)
* **Historical Summary**: Rulers of the Zhodani Consulate's Iakr Sector (Iadr Nsobl Province) from Zdovesil (1212).

### Heptamorphs (Khept-7 Hivemind) (Homeworld Hex `1628`)
* **Morphology**: Radial Heptamorphic Symmetry (7-fold chitinous-neural nodes) (7 articulated locomotory/manipulator appendages with 7 compound-psionic ocelli)
* **Senses & Diet**: 7-Band Ultraviolet/Infrared Ocelli, Heptagonal Synaptic Telepathic Lattice, Magnetoreceptive Resonance | Chemo-organic & biosphere-harvesting
* **Psychology**: Collective distributed hivemind consciousness; views individualist sophonts as chaotic noise
* **Historical Summary**: Evolved on Septima Prime (1628); harvested the worlds of northern Subsector J (creating the Subsector J Barren Anomaly) to grow living spore-dreadnoughts, and launched the Septiman War against the Avalar Consulate in 1104.

### Issugur / Periannath (Halflings - Homo sapiens parvulus) (Homeworld Hex `2835`)
* **Morphology**: Bilateral Diminutive Humaniti (0.95–1.10 m tall, furry leathery soles) (4 limbs (2 nimble arms, 2 unshod Tough-soled legs))
* **Senses & Diet**: Acute Hearing, Quiet-Step Proprioception, Natural Psionic Null-Resonance | Omnivore (Agrarian gourmet; seven traditional meals per day)
* **Psychology**: Peaceable, clannish, pastoral, burrow-dwelling, surprisingly resilient under crisis
* **Historical Summary**: Ancient-transplanted Minor Human Race preserved on Parthinia (3018) and thriving in autonomous agrarian shires at The Shire (2835), Bywater (2834), and Bucklebury (2935).
